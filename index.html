ProteinBackstory

Where your protein came from, and who it's owed to.

Paste a UniProt protein ID and draft a provenance note: the organism, where and when it was collected, who recorded it, what is missing, and who it may be owed to. Nothing is sent to us.

How we work

Three habits run through everything we publish.

  • Facts from the records

    What our tools state comes from public records, with sources. Nothing is guessed, and AI suggestions stay leads until a person checks them.

  • Gaps named honestly

    "Not recorded" is a result. A gap stated plainly is a list of things someone could go and find out.

  • Credit, and what goes back

    Living matter comes from places and people. We name them where we can, and ask what goes back.
