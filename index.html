Biodesign Academy

Insights and tools for learning and practising AI-native biodesign.

Free tools and open papers for designers, students and educators working with proteins and living matter. Each tool runs in your browser and cites the paper that explains its method. The courses and weekly Readings live at biodesign.academy.

Protein Backstory

Where your protein came from, and who it's owed to.

Paste a UniProt protein ID and draft a provenance note: the organism, where and when it was collected, who recorded it, what is missing, and who it may be owed to. Nothing is sent to us.
